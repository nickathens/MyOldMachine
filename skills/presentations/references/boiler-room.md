Boiler Room's identity is a poster you'd see taped to a warehouse door. Black ground, white set block sans, an emergency red that reads as signage. Photography is documentary: bodies in motion, sweat, available light.

Their wordmark carries a directness that film treatments can borrow. The reds are arterial, not lipstick. Composition is a little off-square, never centred for the sake of centering.

A treatment leaning on Boiler Room should keep the black dense, the grain honest, the red used where a sign would be used. Feel like a night, not a launch.
