MUBI is what happens when a cinephile magazine runs a streaming service. Pure black ground, pure white type, and a screaming yellow used as a wayfinding mark. The yellow is never decorative; it marks a CTA, a chapter number, a single tag.

Typography is a modern display face (their own) paired with Graphik for utility and an editorial serif when writing turns long-form. Hero stills are given the space of a painting in a gallery. Nothing competes with the image.

A treatment borrowing from MUBI should think like a curator, not a marketer. One image at a time. Captions that read. No extra colour beyond the single yellow, and only where it actually points at something.
