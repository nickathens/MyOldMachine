The Opus reference represents independent film-print editorial at its most disciplined. One serif family (Caslon or similar old-style) carries every word on the page, at every weight. There is no grotesque. There are no coloured boxes.

The ground is an off-white that sits closer to paper than screen. Black is true. When accent arrives, it is a mossy green or a deep wine — never more than one across the whole document. Pull quotes are large, italicised, and never recoloured.

A treatment leaning on this reference should commit to a single serif, refuse pure white, and carry the whole emotional range inside one type system. This is the quietest reference in the library, and usually the right one when the subject deserves respect.
