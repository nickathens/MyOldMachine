Aesop is a typographic brand that happens to sell skincare. The word itself is set in a bold but tender sans with arched apertures, very close to Optima. Body copy tends toward a calm neo-grotesque with optical sizes that behave well on a label as much as on a wall.

Palette is monochrome by discipline. Amber glass, unbleached paper, ink black. When green appears it is the deep forest of the bottle and nothing louder. Store identity borrows from apothecary and Japanese wabi-sabi: rough finishes, honest materials, long hold times.

Treatments borrowing from Aesop should earn their photography. A single still life will outperform a gallery. Copy leans literary. Negative space is the product. The page should feel like it was composed by a person who reads.
