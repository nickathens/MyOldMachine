Cahiers du Cinéma is the film magazine as argument. The journal-yellow cover became its own colour: a flat, slightly green-leaning warm yellow, usually holding a black-and-white film still and a single line of critic's type. Inside, the pages behave like a thesis — wide columns of serif, essays that outlast the issue.

Type is conservative on purpose. Times or a close neighbour carries the long reading, and any deviation is a contemporary grotesque or a mono used as labeling, never as voice.

A treatment leaning on Cahiers should behave like a critical essay. Long serif columns, left-aligned. A single colour field as cover — yellow is not the only option, but one flat colour is. Photography is always film still, never staged.
