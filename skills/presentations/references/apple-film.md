The Apple film campaigns (for example the Shot on iPhone and Apple Originals work) read as reductive by discipline. The logo is small. The type is small. The image is enormous and silent. Black or white only. Almost no colour blocks.

SF Pro carries all the weight. Occasionally New York makes an appearance for long-form. The accent is the image itself. CTAs are understated — single words, no chrome.

A treatment leaning on Apple should resist the urge to decorate. Make the photograph enormous. Keep the type quieter than instinct. No accent colour at all.
