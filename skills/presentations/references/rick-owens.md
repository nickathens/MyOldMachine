Rick Owens runs on restraint. The palette is concrete, bone, and a blacker-than-black. There is no accent colour that reads as colour; the warm tones you see are dust and dim lamp light. Type is all caps, tight tracking, structural.

The world feels like a monastery renovated by an architect. Surfaces are honest. Photography is interior. Poses are long and still. Chrome is removed.

A treatment borrowing from Rick Owens should refuse decoration, hold negative space like stone, and treat every warm tint as if it is leaking in from a source outside the frame. Type should be small and calm enough that you can hear it.
