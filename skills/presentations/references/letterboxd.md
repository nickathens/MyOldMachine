Letterboxd reads as a film diary kept in good taste. The ground is a flat navy-black, never pure black. Type is a soft contemporary sans. Green is the only strong accent and it appears almost exclusively on the four-star rating system and on friendly system notes.

Photography is secondary to lists. A well-curated list behaves like the cover. Poster images are crushed small and respected; no one page tries to be a gallery.

A treatment leaning on Letterboxd should behave like a diary: long scrolls, quiet type, a friendly accent used only structurally. Avoid treating images as the main event. The list is the art.
