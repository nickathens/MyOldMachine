# Soul V2 (Higgsfield)

Covers: soul (Soul V2)

## Prompt Style

Character-focused, concise descriptions. Soul V2 excels at character consistency and identity preservation. Keep prompts tight and identity-anchored.

## Optimal Length

30-75 words. Shorter prompts with clear identity markers outperform longer ones.

## Key Techniques

- **Identity-first structure.** Lead with who the subject is before what they are doing: "A tall woman with silver-streaked black hair and angular features, wearing a tailored navy coat, standing at a rain-slicked crosswalk."
- **5-point identity tag.** For character consistency across generations, define: age + build + distinctive feature + hair + clothing. Keep these fixed.
- **Action as context, not focus.** Soul handles static poses and mild actions well. Describe actions simply: "leaning against a wall", "looking over her shoulder". Avoid complex multi-person choreography.
- **Style presets.** Soul responds well to named art styles: "oil painting style", "charcoal sketch", "watercolor wash", "digital illustration". One style per prompt.
- **Background simplicity.** Keep environments described in one phrase: "foggy London street", "sunlit kitchen", "empty train platform". The model excels at subject rendering, not scene complexity.

## What NOT to Do

- Don't write prompts over 100 words as the model starts losing coherence on later details
- Don't describe multiple characters with equal weight in one prompt
- Don't use technical photography terms (focal length, aperture) as this is illustration-oriented

## Example Refinement

Bad: "a person in a city, realistic, detailed"
Good: "A lean man in his 40s with close-cropped gray hair and deep-set eyes, wearing a rumpled linen shirt, sitting alone at a sidewalk cafe table. Warm evening light. Cinematic illustration style."
