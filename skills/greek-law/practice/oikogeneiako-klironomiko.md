# Οικογενειακό και Κληρονομικό Δίκαιο

The fourth and fifth books of the Αστικός Κώδικας: family relations and succession on
death. They are paired here because the same practitioner usually carries both, the
claims are civil αγωγές that run through the ordinary courts, and the αοριστία
discipline of `practice/politiki-dikonomia.md` governs every pleading they produce. The
claim bases below are in `scripts/vasi_agogis.py` as element checklists.

One register note before the law. Family matters are the area where a layperson is most
likely to ask, and most exposed when they get it wrong. Set the register from the
question, give the practical path (which court, what to bring, the cost), and escalate
to a δικηγόρος the moment a deadline or contested money or a child's residence is in
play. None of what follows is advice on a particular family.

---

## Οικογενειακό Δίκαιο (Book 4)

The field covers γάμος and its dissolution, σχέσεις των συζύγων, γονική μέριμνα and
επιμέλεια of children, διατροφή, συγγένεια and its establishment, υιοθεσία, and
επιτροπεία. Two claim types dominate practice.

### Διαζύγιο (dissolution of marriage)

Two roads, and they do not mix:

- Συναινετικό διαζύγιο: by agreement, since Ν.4509/2017 it is done by a
  συμβολαιογραφική πράξη, not through a court. There is no αγωγή. If there are minor
  children the spouses also agree on επιμέλεια, επικοινωνία and διατροφή in the same
  act.
- Διαζύγιο με αγωγή, λόγω ισχυρού κλονισμού (ΑΚ 1439): the contested road. The plaintiff
  pleads either facts that shake the marital relationship so strongly that continuing it
  is reasonably intolerable for them (ΑΚ 1439 παρ. 1), or relies on the presumption of
  breakdown that arises from a period of separation (διάσταση) under ΑΚ 1439 παρ. 3.
  Confirm the separation period in the current text before relying on it, since it has
  been a moving figure. The judgment dissolves the marriage once it is αμετάκλητη.

The αοριστία trap is a petition that invokes κλονισμός in the abstract. Plead the
concrete facts, or the start and the continuity of the separation.

### Διατροφή (maintenance)

Several distinct claims share the word. Keep them apart, because the βάση and the
obligor differ:

- Διατροφή ανήλικου τέκνου: the common one. The minor child who cannot maintain itself
  from its own property or work is entitled (ΑΚ 1486). Both parents owe in proportion to
  their means (ΑΚ 1489). The measure is set by the child's needs (ΑΚ 1493), in money,
  payable monthly in advance (ΑΚ 1496). The child sues through the parent who has
  επιμέλεια.
- Διατροφή μεταξύ συζύγων and μετά το διαζύγιο: between spouses (ΑΚ 1391) and the post
  divorce maintenance of a former spouse in the conditions of ΑΚ 1442, which are narrow.
- Διατροφή λόγω συγγένειας: among relatives in the direct line (ΑΚ 1485 κ.ε.).

The recurring αοριστία in a διατροφή αγωγή is a sum demanded without the analysis behind
it. Plead the child's specific needs and the income and property of both parents, so the
proportion the law requires can be computed. A bare figure is ποσοτικά αόριστο.

### What the skill does here, and what it refuses

It drafts and it explains: the two divorce roads, the maintenance claim with its needs
and means analysis, the difference between γονική μέριμνα and επιμέλεια. It does not
counsel on the custody of a particular child or predict a court's welfare assessment,
which turns on the συμφέρον του τέκνου weighed on evidence. That is advocacy and
judgement for the δικηγόρος, not output a model should manufacture.

## Κληρονομικό Δίκαιο (Book 5)

Succession opens at death. The estate passes either by διαθήκη (testate) or, absent a
valid one, εξ αδιαθέτου (intestate, the order of ΑΚ 1813 κ.ε., with the surviving spouse
taking alongside the relatives). An heir acquires automatically at death but may
αποποιηθεί (renounce) within the deadline, and accepts with the benefit of inventory
(επ' ωφελεία απογραφής) to cap liability for estate debts. Two claim bases recur.

### Αγωγή περί κλήρου (ΑΚ 1871)

The heir's claim to recover the estate, or single estate objects, from someone holding
them as if heir (νομέας του κλήρου) on a hereditary right they do not have. Plead the
hereditary capacity and its basis (the will or the intestate call), the defendant's
holding of estate objects under a claimed hereditary right, and the identity of the
objects. The consequence is recognition of the right and απόδοση of the property (ΑΚ
1875 κ.ε. on the extent). It is exposed to the same identity rigour as the διεκδικητική:
describe the objects, above all immovables, with precision.

### Νόμιμη μοίρα and μέμψη άστοργης δωρεάς (ΑΚ 1825, 1835)

Greek law protects close relatives against disinheritance. The αναγκαίοι κληρονόμοι
(descendants, parents, and the surviving spouse) are entitled to a νόμιμη μοίρα equal to
half of their intestate share (ΑΚ 1825), and they take it as heirs by operation of law
(ΑΚ 1829). The μοίρα is computed on a notional estate that adds back gifts the deceased
made in life (ΑΚ 1831, confirm the article). Where lifetime δωρεές impair the μοίρα, the
forced heir reduces them through the μέμψη άστοργης δωρεάς (ΑΚ 1835 κ.ε.).

The αοριστία here is quantitative and unforgiving: state the kinship, the value of the
real and the notional estate, and the shortfall in the μοίρα. Without the computation
the claim cannot be adjudicated.

### Deadlines worth flagging

Succession runs on strict periods: the time to αποποίηση, and the παραγραφή of the αγωγή
περί κλήρου, among others. Treat any date as load bearing, tell the user to confirm the
governing article, and reach for the Stage 4 deadline tool rather than compute from
memory.

## Honest limits

The article numbers and structure here are well settled doctrine, and the skill stands
behind them. The few moving figures are flagged in place (the διάσταση period of ΑΚ 1439,
the computation article of the νόμιμη μοίρα). When the exact wording carries the
argument, fetch the consolidated text (e-nomothesia.gr or the gazette) per the citation
discipline in SKILL.md, and never write a decision citation you have not verified. These
fields decide where a child lives and how an estate divides, so the escalation to a
δικηγόρος is not a formality.
